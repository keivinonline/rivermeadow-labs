# Workshop Pre-requisites

---

1. **AWS Account**

- Participants would need a regular pay-as-you-go account (that is not **NOT** under the `Free account plan` which limits the services and instance types that are required for the workshop)
- Participants will share their AWS Account IDs to Rivermeadow PIC to allow sharing of workshop AMIs

2. **Rivermeadow account**

- Participants would need to have access to their respective Rivermeadow accounts via [migrate.rivermeadow.com](https://migrate.rivermeadow.com) with sufficient entitlements
