# Workshop Pre-requisites

---

## Access

1. **AWS Account**

- Participants would need a regular pay-as-you-go account (that is not **NOT** under the `Free account plan` which limits the services and instance types that are required for the workshop)
- Participants will share their **AWS Account IDs** to Rivermeadow PICs to allow sharing of workshop AMIs

2. **Rivermeadow account**

- Participants would need share their work emails to Rivermeadow PICs to have access to their respective Rivermeadow accounts via [migrate.rivermeadow.com](https://migrate.rivermeadow.com) with sufficient entitlements
